APP NAME (max 30)
============================================================
Retro-C64

SHORT DESCRIPTION (64/80)
============================================================
A Commodore 64 and a real SID chiptune player, in the machine's own colours

FULL DESCRIPTION (2971/4000)
============================================================
Retro-C64 emulates two of the Commodore 64's chips at once, and builds its whole interface out of one of them. It is built on VICE, the emulator that has been the reference for C64 accuracy for over thirty years.

THE VIC-II COLOURS THE APP
Every colour you see is one of the sixteen the VIC-II could produce - Pepto's measurements of the real chip's output, the same values VICE renders with. The blue you are looking at is the C64's blue. Nothing here is a dark theme with a retro name on it.

THE SID IS A SEPARATE EMULATOR
SID tunes are not recordings. They are 6502 code that drives a sound chip, and Retro-C64 runs them the way the machine did - through reSID, on a second, independent emulator core. Because that core is separate from the one running your games, a tune carries on playing while you browse your library, change your settings or read a disk directory. Point the app at a folder of .sid files and it becomes a chiptune player that happens to also be a computer.

WORKS THE MOMENT YOU OPEN IT
The app ships free, open-source ROMs and a small demo program. Together they boot a real emulated Commodore 64 and run it straight away, with no files, no account and no network. You can watch a C64 come up before you decide anything.

LOAD WHAT YOU ALREADY HAVE
Disk images (D64, D71, D81, G64), tapes (TAP, T64), cartridges (CRT), programs (PRG, P00) and SID music. Point the app at a folder on your device and it catalogues what it finds; zipped downloads work as they are, no unpacking required.

BUILT FOR TOUCH AND FOR CONTROLLERS
A full C64 keyboard laid out for the screen, a draggable on-screen joystick you can arrange how you like, and support for Bluetooth and USB gamepads.

FEATURES
- VICE-based Commodore 64 emulation (6510, VIC-II, SID, 1541)
- A separate reSID core for .sid playback, which keeps playing across the app
- An interface drawn entirely in the VIC-II's sixteen colours
- D64, D71, D81, G64, TAP, T64, CRT, PRG, P00 and SID support
- Free open-source ROMs and a demo built in, so it runs out of the box
- On-screen C64 keyboard and a configurable touch joystick
- External gamepad support
- Save states and a library that organises itself

ABOUT COMMODORE'S ROMS
Commercial C64 software was written against Commodore's own BASIC and KERNAL, which are still under copyright and cannot be shipped with any emulator. To run your old games you supply those yourself - dump them from a C64 you own, use a licensed set such as C64 Forever, or copy them from an existing VICE installation. The app files them in the right place automatically, and a zipped ROM set works without unpacking. The built-in open ROMs are an independent reimplementation and will not run most commercial titles.

This app ships with no copyrighted software and no games. Add your own legally owned C64 titles to begin.
