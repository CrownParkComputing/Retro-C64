This is a hardware emulator for the Commodore 64 (1982), permitted under
App Review Guideline 4.7. It ships with no games and no Commodore ROMs.

HOW TO SEE A WORKING EMULATOR IN UNDER A MINUTE, WITH NOTHING TO DOWNLOAD

On the first-run setup screen, tap "See it working". The app installs a
free, open-source ROM set and puts a small demo program in the games list.
Tap "Go to games", then tap "Retro-C64 Demo". A real emulated C64 boots and
runs it.

THE COMPLIANCE PAGE

Open the app and tap "Compliance" in the sidebar. One page, on the device,
needing no network connection, that answers everything this submission
turns on:

  1. A switch that puts the app on the free ROMs. It boots from its own
     ROM folder, so it cannot touch or depend on any ROMs a user has
     installed. The page then says which file to open and where: Games in
     the sidebar, then "Retro-C64 Demo". A real emulated C64 boots and runs
     it, loaded the same way any tape or disk is loaded -- nothing is typed
     in or started for you.
  2. The demo's files are listed by name with their full path, in a folder
     you can open and read -- the free ROMs, their licence texts and the
     demo program itself.
  3. What the free ROMs are, who wrote them and under which licence.
  4. That Commodore's ROMs are never shipped, and the legitimate ways to
     obtain them.
  5. That no games are shipped.
  6. The free-software licences and where the source is.
  7. The privacy position.

There is also a link to it from About.

Setup can be re-run at any time from Paths ("Run setup again"), and its
"Store Compliance" button switches to the free ROMs and starts the demo
immediately.

Nothing else is needed: no files, no accounts, no network, no ROMs of your
own. That path exists specifically so the app can be evaluated by someone
who has no Commodore hardware to take ROMs from.

WHY THE APP ASKS FOR ROMS AT ALL

The C64's own BASIC and KERNAL are still under copyright and cannot legally
be distributed, so commercial C64 software needs the user to supply them,
exactly as with every other C64 emulator. The built-in set used by the demo
is the MEGA65 project's Open ROMs -- an independent, clean-room
reimplementation, not Commodore's code. It boots and runs the demo, but it
is an unfinished project and much commercial software will not run on it,
which is why the app still offers to import a real set.

To evaluate with a real ROM set instead, place one (kernal, basic, chargen,
and dos1541 for disk images) in the app's Documents folder via the Files
app, then use Paths > Scan for ROMs. A zipped set works as-is.

The app never shows a black screen for want of ROMs: without them it shows
setup guidance, and now also the working demo above. Nothing in that state
is a crash or a non-functional screen.

OTHER

No accounts, no login and no server, so no demo account is needed. The app
collects no data at all and sends none anywhere; the privacy policy is at
https://www.crownparkcomputing.com/privacy

The app makes no network request of its own. There is one optional feature
that can: in Settings you may enter a URL of your own for cover artwork, and
only then does the app fetch from it. It is empty by default and nothing is
contacted unless you fill it in.

The app is built on free software (VICE, reSID, Open ROMs). The licences
and the links to the complete source are listed in the app under About >
Licences and source.
